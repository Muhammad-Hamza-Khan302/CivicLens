CivicLens Knowledge Base
Topic: Potholes and Road Damage
Jurisdiction: General Civic Use
Source Type: Civic service and safety guidance

POTHOLE DEFINITION

A pothole is a hole, depression, crack, or damaged section in the surface of a road.

Road damage may occur on:

- Main roads
- Residential streets
- Service roads
- Intersections
- Bridges
- Road shoulders
- Bike lanes
- Other public road surfaces

ROAD DAMAGE CATEGORIES

CivicLens can classify road-related complaints into:

- Pothole
- Cave-in
- Rough or cracked pavement
- Road resurfacing need
- Damage related to road construction
- Damage around infrastructure such as manholes
- Broken or damaged road surface

REPORTING AND RESPONSIBILITY

Road and pothole complaints should be directed to the relevant municipal road, transportation, works, or infrastructure authority.

A deep or hollow roadway collapse is different from an ordinary pothole and may require urgent investigation.

The responsible authority may determine which department, contractor, utility, or organization is responsible for repair.

SAFETY RULE

An unprotected street opening or major roadway collapse that could cause an accident should be treated as an immediate safety concern.

People should contact the appropriate emergency service when the condition creates an immediate danger to life or safety.

CIVICLENS APPLICATION

When analyzing a road complaint:

1. Identify whether the problem is a pothole, cave-in, resurfacing issue, or another street defect.
2. Check the description for immediate safety risks.
3. A deep or hollow roadway collapse should not automatically be classified as an ordinary pothole.
4. Identify the responsible municipal authority when reliable information is available.
5. Consider the size and severity of the road damage.
6. Consider the effect on traffic and pedestrians.
7. Consider whether the issue is recurring.
8. Escalate cases involving immediate danger or major roadway collapse.

IMPORTANT RULE

CivicLens must not claim a specific repair timeframe as an official requirement unless the timeframe is supported by an applicable official government source.
