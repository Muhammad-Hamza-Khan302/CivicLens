CivicLens AI
Document Type: Civic Issue Priority Decision Framework
Jurisdiction Context: General Civic Use

Important: This document is a CivicLens decision-support framework, not an official government priority policy.

PURPOSE

CivicLens uses this framework to estimate the priority of a civic complaint based on safety risk, severity, public impact, issue type, and available municipal response information.

PRIORITY LEVELS

Priority 1 - CRITICAL

Use when there is an immediate or serious safety risk.

Examples:
- Dangerous street opening that could cause an accident
- Major roadway collapse or dangerous cave-in
- Fallen streetlight pole
- Exposed electrical hazard
- Immediate danger from storm flood water

Recommended action:
- Escalate immediately
- Identify the responsible emergency or municipal agency
- Do not treat the complaint as a routine maintenance request

Priority 2 - HIGH

Use when the issue creates a significant public safety or infrastructure risk but is not an immediate emergency.

Examples:
- Severe road damage affecting traffic
- Major streetlight failure in a high-use location
- Serious drainage or sewer problem
- Large illegal dumping affecting public access
- Repeated civic issue at the same location

Recommended action:
- Prioritize municipal review
- Identify the responsible agency
- Consider location, severity, and public exposure

Priority 3 - MEDIUM

Use for problems that affect public infrastructure or quality of life but do not indicate an immediate safety emergency.

Examples:
- Ordinary pothole
- Streetlight outage without emergency electrical danger
- Sidewalk condition
- Street condition
- Water maintenance issue
- Ordinary sewer maintenance issue

Recommended action:
- Create or route a service request
- Follow the normal agency response process
- Use available municipal response-time information

Priority 4 - LOW

Use for minor issues with limited immediate public impact.

Examples:
- Minor cosmetic street condition
- Small non-hazardous cleanliness issue
- Minor maintenance issue with no immediate safety risk

Recommended action:
- Route through normal civic service channels
- Monitor status

FACTORS USED BY CIVICLENS

Priority should consider:

1. Safety risk
2. Severity of physical damage
3. Public exposure
4. Location
5. Potential effect on traffic or pedestrians
6. Environmental or sanitation impact
7. Whether the issue is recurring
8. Whether the issue involves an emergency condition
9. Available municipal response-time information

IMPORTANT RULE

CivicLens should not claim that a priority score is an official government classification unless an official government source explicitly provides that classification.

The system should explain why a complaint was classified as Critical, High, Medium, or Low.

The system should provide the supporting policy or guideline retrieved from the knowledge base.

CivicLens should use the location and available government information to identify the appropriate responsible agency when reliable information is available.
